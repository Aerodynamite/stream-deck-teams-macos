# Permit workplace use and reserve paid redistribution

Decision recorded: 2026-10-04, based on the publication interview answers through Q16.

The plugin will be free to use, including at work, and people may modify and share it for free. Selling copies, selling modified versions, or including a copy in a paid bundle requires Heartbit's permission; paid installation, configuration, and support are allowed, as are paid profiles that link to the official free download. This boundary permits ordinary professional use and paid assistance while reserving the sale of the software itself, which is why neither unrestricted resale nor a blanket prohibition on commercial use expresses the publisher's intent.

This is an accepted licensing policy, not a license grant or selection of legal text. The exact license, copyright holder, contributor permissions, and treatment of third-party components remain to be resolved before applying terms to the repository.
